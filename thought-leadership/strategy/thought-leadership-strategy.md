# Assembled.sg Thought Leadership Strategy

## Executive Summary

This strategy positions **Assembled** — Singapore's best-rated market research agency — as the go-to thought leader for consumer intelligence in Singapore and Southeast Asia. The goal is to convert Assembled's deep operational expertise (600+ projects, 100,000-member proprietary panel, bilingual moderation capability) into visible authority that drives inbound leads from multinational brands entering or expanding in the region.

---

## 1. Core Thought Leadership Pillars

### Pillar 1: The Singapore Consumer Decoded
**Theme:** No one understands the Singapore consumer better than Assembled.
- Cultural nuances that break global campaigns (Singlish, face culture, kiasu behaviour)
- Generational divides: Gen Z vs Pioneer Generation spending psychology
- The multicultural consumer: Malay, Chinese, Indian, and Eurasian segments
- HDB heartland vs CBD: two Singapores, two consumer mindsets

### Pillar 2: Market Entry Intelligence
**Theme:** De-risk your Singapore launch with local ground truth.
- Why desk research fails in Singapore (high-context market dynamics)
- The real cost of getting Singapore wrong (case studies from F&B, fintech, beauty)
- Southeast Asia is not one market — country-by-country consumer differences
- Regulatory and cultural tripwires foreign brands miss

### Pillar 3: Research Methodology Leadership
**Theme:** Assembled invented The Singapore Insights Method for a reason.
- Why proprietary panels beat purchased databases
- Bilingual moderation as a competitive advantage
- Focus groups vs surveys: when to use what in Singapore
- Ethnography and mystery shopping for authentic consumer behaviour
- Mobile ethnography: capturing real-time consumer decisions

### Pillar 4: Industry-Specific Consumer Intelligence
**Theme:** Deep vertical expertise across Singapore's key sectors.
- Healthcare: Patient journey research and HCP engagement
- F&B: Foodie culture, halal certification, food delivery behaviour
- Beauty & Personal Care: Skincare preferences across ethnic segments
- Financial Services: Insurance, wealth management, banking behaviours
- FMCG: Premium consumers and brand switching triggers

## 8. The "State of the Singapore Consumer" Annual Report

This is the flagship thought leadership asset. A comprehensive annual report based on Assembled's proprietary panel data that becomes the definitive reference for anyone making consumer decisions in Singapore.

**Proposed sections:**
1. Consumer confidence and spending sentiment
2. Category deep-dives (F&B, beauty, healthcare, finance, tech)
3. Generational analysis (Gen Z, Millennials, Gen X, Boomers)
4. Multicultural consumer trends
5. Digital behaviour and channel preferences
6. Predictions for the year ahead

**Distribution:** Free executive summary, full report gated behind email capture. Promoted via LinkedIn, email, PR, and industry events.

This single asset can generate 20+ derivative content pieces (blog posts, LinkedIn posts, infographics, speaking topics) throughout the year.
